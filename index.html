About TriageTime

What this is

A free, independent tool showing hospital waiting times across Australia. No paywall. No signup. No tracking.

Data Sources

  • QLD Live: Queensland Health — CC BY 4.0
  • NSW Live: NSW Health via ed-wait-log
  • Annual (all states): AIHW via publicdata.au — CC BY 4.0

What the columns mean

  • Waiting Now — the number of people who have been triaged and are waiting to be seen by a doctor.
  • Current Wait — the median time from arrival to being seen, across all triage categories.
  • Updated — when the source last refreshed this hospital's data.
