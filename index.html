About TriageTime

What this is

A free, independent tool showing hospital waiting times across Australia. No paywall. No signup. No tracking.

Data Sources

  • QLD Live: Queensland Health — CC BY 4.0
  • Annual (all states): AIHW via publicdata.au — CC BY 4.0

What the columns mean

  • Waiting Now — the number of people who have been triaged and are waiting to be seen by a doctor.
  • Average Wait — the median time from arrival to being seen. Half of people wait less, half wait more.
  • Updated — when the source last refreshed this hospital's data.

Why no live data for NSW, WA, VIC and SA?

NSW Health publishes live data through an API that doesn't offer a stable CSV download. WA Health publishes as an HTML table only. VIC and SA do not publish live ED data at all. Their historical data is available in the Historical Data tab.
