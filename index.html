About TriageTime

What this is

A free, independent tool that shows hospital waiting times across Australia. No paywall. No signup. No tracking.

Data Sources

  • QLD Live: Queensland Health — CC BY 4.0
  • NSW Live: NSW Health
  • WA Live: WA Health
  • AU Annual (ED, Surgical, 4-Hour): AIHW via publicdata.au — CC BY 4.0

What the numbers mean

  • Waiting Now — the number of people who have been triaged and are waiting to be seen by a doctor.
  • Median Wait — the typical wait time. Half of people wait less, half wait more.
  • % Seen On Time — the percentage of patients seen within the target time for their triage category.
  • % Left Within 4 Hrs — the percentage of ED patients who completed their visit and left within 4 hours of arrival.

Why no live data for VIC and SA?

The Victorian and South Australian governments do not publish live or near-real-time emergency department waiting times for public hospitals. Their data is only available in annual or quarterly reports. We show their historical data in the section above.
